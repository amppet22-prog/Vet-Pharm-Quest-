## Source-verified teaching rules from retrieved Exam 2 excerpts
- Mixed-diameter intestinal populations can **favor** mechanical obstruction but do not prove it; a proximal lesion and repeated vomiting can obscure typical patterns.
- Linear foreign body may cause bowel plication and small abnormal gas pockets *without* dramatic dilation.
- If small-bowel segments are variably dilated, functional ileus versus early/partial obstruction can remain unresolved; don't force a definitive answer without support.
- A mass-effect interpretation may be possible while organ of origin remains indeterminate; distinguish small bowel and colon using orthogonal views.
- Loss of abdominal serosal detail and heterogeneous other signs may require ultrasound and/or fluid assessment.

These are paraphrased **educational concepts**, not public copies of original notes or images.
