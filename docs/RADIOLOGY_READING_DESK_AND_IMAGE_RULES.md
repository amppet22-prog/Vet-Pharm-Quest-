# Veterinary Radiology Reading Desk — Phase 1 (2026-11-10)

## What is live in the repository
- `radiology-reading-desk.js`, loaded by `index.html` and available through **Examination Room → Radiology reading desk**.
- Twelve ungraded technique/quality/reporting questions on collimation, correct anatomy, positioning, projections, kVp, mAs, motion and ALARA.
- A structured report-writing practice interface: study/view, technical quality, normal/abnormal status, objective findings, prioritized differential diagnoses, limitations and next steps.
- Educational guide to VD vs DV, lateral identification, kVp vs mAs and examination quality.
- No radiographic image has been uploaded or copied into the app at this stage. Text-only image-interpretation questions must not be represented as diagnostic reading of a film.
- The existing clinic save and XP/coins are untouched.

## Image library inclusion criteria
1. Original DICOM, or a sufficiently clear original export for educational interpretation, not a tiny image embedded in a lecture screenshot.
2. Grounded species, region, projection and laterality, clinical history if available.
3. Verified author/radiologist-approved key; technical quality and normal/abnormal class, objective lesion description, differentials, and key caveats.
4. Redistribution permission for a publicly accessible GitHub educational game; citation alone does not license copyrighted images.
5. Dedicated image-source credit attached to each film and a separate credits index.
6. Show multiple complementary views when the clinical interpretation requires them. No invented image findings.

## External study references, *not* republished case images
- Radio Vyra public browse/explore: https://radiovista.app/browse.html . Public interface lists veterinary medicine as beta. Check image inventory and licensing individually.
- International Veterinary Radiology Association OER: https://www.ivraimaging.org/oer-open-education-resources . Teaching summaries and case links, subject to individual rights.
- American College of Veterinary Radiology (ACVR): https://acvr.org/how-we-do-it/types-of-imaging-therapy/radiology/ . Professional guidance and examples, generally protected by terms requiring authorization for republication.
- ACVR radiation safety: https://acvr.org/how-we-do-it/types-of-imaging-therapy/radiology/acvrs-radiation-safety-statement/
- ACVR/ECVDI reporting foundation: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/vru.13471
- Student-uploaded Thrall chapter 7 (appendicular skeleton) and clinical imaging lecture/OSCE references: review source images and permissions before adding.
