The future large-animal case area will include equine, bovine, small-ruminant and porcine patients. Case selection, lectures and study tools will not depend on player level. Decorative animals will use each learner's purchased companion IDs and optional personal nicknames when that feature is implemented.

This prototype uses sample placeholders and one already-present horse illustration. It does not use or change the live companion renderer or account save system. Room-placement rules and ownership checks will be developed and tested separately.
